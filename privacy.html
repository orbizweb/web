Operational Integrity

Enterprise Privacy &
Data Governance Protocols.

Last updated: September 2026. Orbiz.one is committed to absolute intellectual property ownership, rigorous international compliance, and automated cybersecurity enforcement.

1. Strategic Commitment & Scope

Orbiz.one provides strategic digital engineering, Global Capability Center (GCC) establishment, talent pod augmentation, and proprietary AI accelerators to enterprises worldwide. Because our clients entrust us with mission-critical systems and proprietary technology roadmaps, data protection and confidentiality are not ancillary policies—they are foundational to our Digital Craftsmanship ethos.

2. 100% Intellectual Property (IP) Guarantee

Under our standard Master Services Agreement (MSA) and engagement contracts:

  • • Exclusive Client Ownership: All source code, architectural blueprints, database designs, API schemas, product documentation, and intellectual assets created during an engagement belong 100% and exclusively to the client immediately upon creation.
  • • No Residual Claims: Neither Orbiz.one nor its assigned engineering personnel retain any lien, license, or claim over custom technology developed for clients.
  • • Ironclad NDAs: All engineers and administrative personnel execute strict, perpetual non-disclosure and invention assignment agreements prior to project assignment.

3. Automated Compliance via CloudAudit AI

We deploy our proprietary CloudAudit AI agent across development environments, workstations, and cloud delivery pipelines to conduct continuous automated security verification:

  • ✓ GDPR & DPDP: Rigorous personal data pseudonymization, lawful basis processing, and full support for data subject access and deletion requests.
  • ✓ SOC2 Type II Controls: Role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), least-privilege credentialing, and comprehensive immutable audit trails.
  • ✓ HIPAA Technical Safeguards: End-to-end data encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3) for clients in the healthcare and life sciences sectors.

4. Data Collection & Website Usage

When interacting with the public orbiz.one website:

  • • Information You Provide: Information submitted via consultation requests or email inquiries (such as your name, corporate email address, and project specifications) is used exclusively to respond to your inquiry and evaluate engagement feasibility.
  • • Zero Third-Party Data Sale: We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your corporate or personal data to third-party brokers or advertisers.
  • • Cookies & Local Storage: Our site uses minimal local browser storage strictly to remember user accessibility preferences (such as light/dark theme toggle state). We do not employ cross-site tracking cookies.
